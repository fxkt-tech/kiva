Longsword training · keyboard & mouse
Left click: horizontal · Right click: vertical
Hold either: charge sword energy
Both buttons / G: parry · C + right click: uppercut
WASD: move · Shift: tap dodge / long-press sprint

Space: double jump · C: toggle crouch / slide
Ctrl: walk · Alt: free look
